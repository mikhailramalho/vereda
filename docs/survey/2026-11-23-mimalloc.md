# mimalloc in place of glibc's malloc

Recorded 2026-11-23.

Issue #68: glibc's malloc and free took over a third of the walk on
array_loop_summary_decrement_fail before #66. The binary now links mimalloc
3.7.0 (its object file, built with `MI_USE_CXX` so that `operator new`
throws `std::bad_alloc`), as ESBMC does since esbmc/esbmc#10056, and glibc's
malloc stays when `MIMALLOC_SOURCE_DIR` is empty or, without
DOWNLOAD_DEPENDENCIES, not given.

The walk gets up to 30% faster and the solve 4 to 45%, since LLVM,
Bitwuzla and CaDiCaL all allocate through it. Peak memory falls on the
tests that use the most (decrement_fail by 220 MB) and grows by 8 to 37 MB
on the rest, mimalloc's fixed cost. Under perf, the allocator's share of
the whole decrement_fail run falls from 12.1% (malloc_consolidate,
_int_malloc, ...) to 5.1%.

Thirteen of the slowest ReachSafety tasks of the 2026-11-23 baseline, with
`--incremental-bmc` and the property file, 120 s each, side by side: the
eight that end take 4 to 41% less wall time (memory-model/2SB 108.8 s to
64.6 s, egcd3-ll_valuebound10 107.8 s to 88.9 s, geo1-ll_valuebound10
17.2 s to 14.0 s, reducercommutativity/max10-1 36.8 s to 35.2 s) with the
same verdicts; the five that time out do so with both.

## Under the memory limit

The tests cap address space (RLIMIT_AS, 4 GB), and mimalloc keeps the
address space of what it frees, so out of the box it runs out with much less
resident than glibc. Three changes bring it back to glibc's behaviour.

mimalloc reserved its first arena, 1 GiB, before main() (LLVM's static
constructors allocate), so setting the arena size at run time, as ESBMC
does, came too late. The 64 MiB arena size is now built in
(`MI_DEFAULT_ARENA_RESERVE`).

esbmc-cstd/scanf-false-3 (CORE), which times out at 300 s on main, ran out
of memory on the pull request's first CI run after 110 s. At the failure the
process held 4.08 GB of address space and 2.44 GB resident; the allocation
that failed was CaDiCaL growing a vector to 512 MiB. mimalloc puts each
object up to 2 GiB in an arena, reserving a new arena that fits when none
does, and keeps that arena after the object is freed: five 256 MiB arenas
held 2 to 71 MB each. With glibc, an object that large is a mapping of its
own, unmapped on free, and main finishes bound 4 at 2.7 GB and runs bound 5
until the timeout. Objects over 32 MiB now get their own mapping too
(`MI_DEFAULT_ARENA_MAX_OBJECT_SIZE`; glibc's mmap threshold grows to the same
32 MiB). Under the limit the branch then finishes bound 4 at 2.6 GB (182 s
against main's 259 s) and times out in bound 5, as main does;
esbmc/struct_array_ite_new_index (KNOWNBUG), which also went from a timeout
to out of memory in that CI run, times out again. An arena size of 0 or
16 MiB did not help (out of memory at 1.6 and 2.6 GB). The threshold costs
page faults (110,000 against 7,400 on decrement_fail, main 580,000) but no
user instructions; decrement_fail's CPU time went from 27.0 s to 29.6 s
against main's 36.3 s, and two more side-by-side runs of the list above
(under a heavier load) gave decrement_fail 37.0 s to 30.6 s, 12_lms_new 132 s
to 95 s, github_302 104 s to 84 s and struct_array_ite_new_index_fail 44 s
to 24 s, with no regression.

On 23_picosat-846_01 the walk, which with glibc runs out at 3.97 GB, ended
at 3.0 GB; a 128 MiB allocation in CaDiCaL then failed during a query the
walker makes, camada turned the `std::bad_alloc` into an unknown answer, and
the next query crashed (SIGSEGV) on the half-updated solver. run.py did not
see that as out of memory: no message, and short of 90% of the limit. The
driver now sets a new-handler that prints `vereda: out of memory` and exits,
with either allocator (with glibc this test now prints it at 3.97 GB instead
of an uncaught `std::bad_alloc`).

With all three, the eight tests that run out of memory on main's CI (the
array_loop_summary family, github_9785_int_base_stores, 23_picosat-846_01)
report OUT OF MEMORY, and sooner (23_picosat-846_01 after 21 s instead of
65 s, at 3.35 GB resident). Over the first CI run, the 6269 tests that end
the same way on main and the branch took 714 s on main and 622 s on the
branch; the 14 among them over 3 s, 252 s and 180 s.
