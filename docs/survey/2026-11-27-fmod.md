# fmod from fp.rem

Recorded 2026-11-27.

frem, which clang emits for C's fmod and which `libmToIntrinsics` now makes
of bodyless fmod, fmodf and fmodl calls, truncates its quotient; SMT-LIB's
fp.rem rounds it to nearest (IEEE remainder). The encoder takes r = fp.rem(x,
y) and, when r is nonzero and its sign is not x's, moves it by |y| towards
x's sign. That sum is exact, as fmod's result is representable, and NaN,
infinite x, zero y and infinite y need no special case. The formula gave
glibc's fmod and fmodf on 20 million random float and double pairs and on
every pair of special values (signed zeros, infinities, NaN, subnormals,
quotients near 1e299).

On unconstrained float operands Bitwuzla's own fp.rem is slow: proving that
fmodf(x, y) has x's sign did not finish in 120 s, and |fmodf(x, y)| <= |x|
took 80 s, while camada's bit-vector encoding (`--fp2bv`) proved all the
properties of `tests/vereda/fmod_nondet.c` in about 15 s. That test runs
under `--fp2bv` only; operands narrowed to a few values take seconds either
way. Five imported tests now give ESBMC's verdict and are CORE; the slowest,
`esbmc-bitwuzla/ieee_rem_fmod_bound`, takes about 90 s.

Later the same day (#445), natively, frem is computed in camada's
bit-vector encoding instead, as ESBMC's Bitwuzla backend does for fp.rem:
the operands go to bit-vectors and the result comes back. Measured on the
main machine, one core each, CPU time of the whole run:

| Test | Native fp.rem | fp.rem in BV, adjustment native | All in BV (adopted) | `--fp2bv` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `vereda/fmod_nondet` | over 600 s | 178 s | 22 s | 16 s |
| `esbmc-bitwuzla/ieee_rem_fmod_bound` | 90 s | 2.2 s | 2.0 s | 2.8 s |
| `esbmc-bitwuzla/ieee_rem_fmod_sign` | 19 s | | 0.8 s | |
| `esbmc-bitwuzla/ieee_rem_fmod_sign_fail` | 15.5 s | | 36 s | |

So the time went to fp.rem's word-blasting and, on unconstrained operands,
also to the adjustment around it in the native theory (comparisons of the
remainder with zero, and the sum with |y|): moving only fp.rem left
fmod_nondet at 178 s. Computing the adjustment in the bit-vector encoding
too brings it to 22 s, close to `--fp2bv`'s 16 s, and fmod_nondet now runs
natively as well. The one loss is the failing sign test, which finds its
counterexample in 36 s instead of 15.5 s. `--fp2bv` runs are unchanged.
